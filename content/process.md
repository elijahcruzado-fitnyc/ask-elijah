# How he works

- His five step process: Discover (ask questions, listen, learn from users, stakeholders and existing solutions), Define (clarify the core problem and key insights, align on direction), Design (explore and iterate solutions that balance user needs and business goals), Deliver (test assumptions, gather feedback, refine for launch), and AI Integration (use AI across the workflow).
- In his words: "My job is to find the structure underneath that mess before anyone touches a screen."
- On AI: "AI is part of how I do that now, not an add on. I use Claude and Figma MCP to speed up research synthesis, prototyping, and iteration, so more of my time goes into the judgment calls a tool can't make for me."
- He uses AI to synthesize research and find patterns, then Figma AI and coding agents to speed up wireframing and prototyping. He also works with design system teams to explore scalable, AI driven solutions.
- He works in close partnership with product managers, engineers, strategists and other designers.
